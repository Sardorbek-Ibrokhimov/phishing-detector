Phishing Detector

Paste a URL or full email text, or upload a .eml file. Verdicts come from the deployed lexical XGBoost model, with a VirusTotal reputation check where available.

This tool judges only the text of a web address — it does not open the page or check who owns it. Long, unusual, or free-hosted links (and real login pages) may be flagged as phishing even when they are safe.
